Graphs:

Graphs are basically connections between the nodes. Unlike trees where we have a root from where all children originates, in graphs each nodes are equal , there is no any parent , its just connections

Terms in Graphs :

Vertex - a node
Edge - connection between nodes
Weighted/Unweighted : values assigned to distances between vertices
Directed/Undirected : directions assigned to distanced between vertices

Undirected and directed graphs:

There is no directions associated with the undirected graph and in directed graph the directions are associated. Let's say A, B , C follows E in instagram and E (can be a celebrity) doesn't follow any of 3 , so it is directed

Weighted graph:

When there is a value on the edges of vertices its weighted graph. Eg : Maps, where distance is the value here to identify shortest path

Storing Graphs:
Adjacency Matrix:

We cannot store all the nodes in the graph instead we can store what is connected to what using adjacency matrix. Its like a two dimensional table where we store the connections in boolean. Eg: If A is connected to B and F , in the 2D table under the row A check for column D and F and put the value as 1 and for other columns B, C, E put the values 0.
